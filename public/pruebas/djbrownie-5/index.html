"Brownie" is Chris Brown. The rest is craft: read the floor and never let it stop.

  1. Preheat in an after-hours

    Start spinning over thirty years ago in the original dark and dirty underground and after-hours venues of Los Angeles.

  2. Spread across the map

    Take the mix on the road for years across the United States, Mexico and Canada.

  3. Knead in the studio

    In the early 90s, start producing with long-time friend Paul Grogan, owner of Rampant Records.

  4. Sign with several names

    Gorilla Pimp, Immigrant, Faithealer, Mr Funkster, Brownie and DJ Brownie. In 1999, bake "Like This" on Rampant.

  5. Serve on screen

    Write for television, film, video games and advertising, without leaving the breaks and bass labels.

  6. Glaze with "Effect"

    October 2025, with SluG (FL) on Kaleidoscope Music. Listen on Beatport ↗
