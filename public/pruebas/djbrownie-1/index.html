Who is DJ Brownie

DJ Brownie is Chris Brown, DJ and producer. He started spinning over thirty years ago in the original dark and dirty underground and after-hours venues of Los Angeles, which led to years of bookings across the United States, Mexico and Canada.

In the early nineties he turned his hand to production with long-time friend Paul Grogan, owner of Rampant Records, releasing under a handful of aliases: Gorilla Pimp, Immigrant, Faithealer, Mr Funkster, Brownie and, of course, DJ Brownie.

Today he's dropping dancefloor bombs on top breaks and bass labels, while also producing for TV, film, video games and commercials.

The story in three bars

The after-hours

Los Angeles, late eighties and early nineties. Warehouses, basements and clubs that opened when everything else shut. That's where Brownie learnt to read a floor at five in the morning.

The studio

With Paul Grogan and Rampant Records he went from playing records to making them. Several aliases, one obsession: the broken beat. In 1999 "Like This" came out on Rampant.

The screen and the floor

These days he splits his time between breaks and bass labels and music for TV, film, games and ads. In October 2025 he released "Effect" with SluG (FL).

Why Brownie? Because it's dense, dark and addictive. Like proper breaks.
