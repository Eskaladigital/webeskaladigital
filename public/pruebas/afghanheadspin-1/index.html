1From Resonant Evil
to KILLA

Afghan Headspin is Jason Bull. The name started in 2006 as a project of Mackie (Jason Bull) and Wrisk (Colin Worth), both from Resonant Evil, the early-2000s British drum & bass trio completed by Audio (Gareth Greenall). When the trio stopped working as a regular group, Mackie and Wrisk opened Afghan Headspin for breaks, drum & bass, dubstep and whatever else they wanted to make.

Going Down came out on Skint on 9 May 2011, billed as Afghan Headspin featuring MC Coppa. On 25 November 2022 Soundsystem Bassrock released No One Dies, an eighteen-track album.

Today Bull is label manager and A&R at Dirty Kitchen Rave. The records sit in breakbeat, UK bass and rave; drum & bass and hardcore are the roots, not the day-to-day output.
