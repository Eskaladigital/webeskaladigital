The label

A multi-genre bass label for bassheads, ravers and late-night misfits.

DIRTY KITCHEN RAVE puts out music from right across the bass spectrum: breaks, UK bass, UK garage, bassline, rave, jungle, drum & bass, breakbeat, dubstep and disco. Genre borders are there to be crossed.

Label manager Afghan Headspin leads a team of UK and international producers, many from the Spanish breaks scene. Around the releases: a podcast, Twitch streams, a Discord community, merch and vinyl.
