About the label

A full-throttle kitchen for bassheads and late-night misfits.

DIRTY KITCHEN RAVE is a multi-genre bass label based in London. It releases whatever hits hardest on a dancefloor, from 134 BPM breaks to half-time garage and full-speed jungle, and refuses to pick just one lane.

Label manager Afghan Headspin runs a crew of UK and international producers, many from the Spanish breaks scene. More than 230 catalogue numbers in, the kitchen also runs a podcast, Twitch streams, a Discord, merch and vinyl.

House rules Posted by management
  1. Bass comes first.If it doesn't move a dancefloor, it doesn't leave the kitchen.
  2. No genre is off the menu.Breaks, UK bass, UKG, bassline, rave, jungle, DnB, breakbeat, dubstep and disco.
  3. Every demo gets tasted.Submissions through LabelRadar go straight to the pass.
  4. The door stays open.Podcast, Twitch streams and a free Discord for the whole crew.
  5. Wear the uniform.Official merch, EU store and vinyl.
