    // DIRTY KITCHEN RAVE

    Label manager
    & A&R

    Dirty Kitchen Rave is the centre of the recent work. Jason Bull runs it as label manager and A&R, and since 2023 the Afghan Headspin name has been on the label almost without a pause.

    The records of these years sit in breakbeat, UK bass and rave. Drum & bass and hardcore are the roots, not the day-to-day output.
