7 homepage proposals · aka Jason Bull

Breakbeat, UK bass and rave, spun seven ways

Seven visual directions for Afghan Headspin's new website. All of them are new, and all of them make sound: open each one and have a play.

01With voice

Rave Hotline

A photocopied rave flyer and a payphone. Press a key: DTMF tones, then a British voice reads the info line over a break squashed through a phone line.

Open demo →
02Interactive

Genre Triangle

Breakbeat, UK bass and rave on the corners of a triangle. Drag the puck and the beat morphs live. Even the logo changes weight. Plus a map of labels and collaborators.

Open demo →
03Hold & drop

Pressure

A speaker stack. Hold to build the pressure, release to drop: sub impact, screen shake and cones that pump on every kick. The sound as a technical rider.

Open demo →
04Spin it

Headspin

An upside-down b-boy inside a ring of letters. Flick it and they spin together, and the faster it spins the more the break opens up. The career as a battle bracket.

Open demo →
05Insert tape

VHS Warehouse

A CRT telly, a VCR and five tapes, one per era: Resonant Evil, 2006, the Skint years, No One Dies and Dirty Kitchen Rave. Rewind, fast-forward, pause.

Open demo →
06Real music

Flyer & Info Line

The 90s photocopied flyer, with a NOW PLAYING hero, the tracklist pulled live from SoundCloud and a sticky player bar. Then the latest mix, the key releases, a wall of gig flyers and the payphone as the booking line: press 1 and it drops you into the music.

Open demo →
07Real music

AH-TV

A 90s music channel. Music channels play the real SoundCloud while the telly shows warehouse footage pulsing to the beat, with the track title on the lower third. Info channels are teletext pages, Ceefax style: about, releases, dates and Dirty Kitchen Rave.
