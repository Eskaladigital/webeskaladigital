DJ Brownie is Chris Brown, DJ and producer. He started spinning over thirty years ago in the original dark and dirty underground and after-hours venues of Los Angeles, the ones that opened when everything else had shut.

That start led to years of bookings across the United States, Mexico and Canada. In the early nineties he went into the studio with his long-time friend Paul Grogan, owner of Rampant Records, and began releasing under several names: Gorilla Pimp, Immigrant, Faithealer, Mr Funkster, Brownie and DJ Brownie.

Today he drops dancefloor bombs on top breaks and bass labels, and scores TV, film, video games and commercials.

Reading a floor at five in the morning isn't something you learn anywhere else.The Brownie school: the Los Angeles after-hours.
