The label

DKR isn't a genre. It's a temperature.

DIRTY KITCHEN RAVE is a multi-genre bass label based in London, built for bassheads, ravers and anyone still dancing at the end of the night. It pushes against genre borders on purpose: a 134 BPM breaks cut can sit next to a half-time garage roller and a jungle tearout on the same compilation.

Label manager Afghan Headspin leads a crew of UK and international producers, with strong roots in the Spanish breaks scene. More than 230 catalogue numbers in, the label also runs a podcast, Twitch streams, a Discord community, merch and vinyl.
