Who's behind
the dial

DJ Brownie is Chris Brown, DJ and producer. He's been spinning for over thirty years: he started in the original dark and dirty underground and after-hours venues of Los Angeles, which led to years of bookings across the United States, Mexico and Canada.

In the early nineties he started producing with his long-time friend Paul Grogan, owner of Rampant Records. He released under many names: Gorilla Pimp, Immigrant, Faithealer, Mr Funkster, Brownie and DJ Brownie. In 1999 "Like This" came out on Rampant.

Today he releases on breaks and bass labels and writes for television, film, video games and advertising. His latest record, "Effect", came out with SluG (FL) on Kaleidoscope Music in October 2025.

Three decades changing frequency without ever losing the signal: the broken beat.
