What's cooking

DIRTY KITCHEN RAVE is a record label for bassheads, ravers and late-night misfits.

DKR puts out music from every corner of the bass spectrum, from 134 BPM breaks to half-time garage and full-speed jungle, without asking which box it fits in. If it moves a dancefloor, it's on the menu.

Run by label manager Afghan Headspin, the label mixes UK producers with a wide international crew, many of them from the Spanish breaks scene. Around the releases there's a whole kitchen: a podcast, live streams on Twitch, a Discord for the community, merch and vinyl.
