    Label

    DIRTY KITCHEN RAVE is a record label for bassheads, ravers and late-night misfits.

    DKR releases across the whole bass spectrum and ignores genre borders on purpose. A 134 BPM breaks cut, a half-time garage roller and a jungle tearout can share the same compilation.

    Label manager Afghan Headspin runs a roster of UK and international producers, many from the Spanish breaks scene.

    Beyond the releases: a podcast, live streams on Twitch, a Discord community, merch and vinyl.
