The kitchen

For bassheads, ravers and late-night misfits.

DIRTY KITCHEN RAVE is a multi-genre bass label based in London. It doesn't ask which box a track fits in: 134 BPM breaks, half-time garage and full-speed jungle all come out of the same kitchen.

Head chef and label manager Afghan Headspin runs a brigade of UK and international producers, many from the Spanish breaks scene. More than 230 catalogue numbers in, with a podcast, Twitch streams, a Discord, merch and vinyl on the side.
