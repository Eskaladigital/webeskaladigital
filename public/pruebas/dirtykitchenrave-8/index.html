The label

For bassheadsand misfits

DIRTY KITCHEN RAVE is a multi-genre bass label based in London. It releases whatever hits hardest on a dancefloor, across ten genres from disco to jungle.

Label manager Afghan Headspin runs a roster of UK and international producers, many from the Spanish breaks scene. More than 230 catalogue numbers so far.

Around the releases: a podcast, Twitch streams, a Discord community, merch and vinyl.
