// LINER NOTES

Afghan Headspin
is Jason Bull

The name started in 2006 as a project of Mackie (Jason Bull) and Wrisk (Colin Worth). Both came from Resonant Evil, the early-2000s British drum & bass trio completed by Audio (Gareth Greenall). Around 2005–2006 the trio stopped working as a regular group, and Mackie and Wrisk opened Afghan Headspin as a vehicle for breaks, drum & bass, dubstep and whatever else they wanted to make.

Going Down came out on Skint on 9 May 2011, billed as Afghan Headspin featuring MC Coppa. On 25 November 2022 Soundsystem Bassrock released No One Dies, an eighteen-track album.

Today he is label manager and A&R at Dirty Kitchen Rave, the centre of the recent work. The records sit in breakbeat, UK bass and rave; drum & bass and hardcore are the roots. The same period ties the project to Spain, and to Andalusia in particular.
