Who's on the system

Afghan Headspin
is Jason Bull

The name started in 2006 as a project of Mackie (Jason Bull) and Wrisk (Colin Worth). Both came from Resonant Evil, the early-2000s British drum & bass trio completed by Audio (Gareth Greenall). When the trio stopped working as a regular group, Mackie and Wrisk opened Afghan Headspin for breaks, drum & bass, dubstep and whatever else they wanted to make.

Going Down came out on Skint on 9 May 2011, billed as Afghan Headspin featuring MC Coppa. On 25 November 2022 Soundsystem Bassrock released No One Dies, an eighteen-track album.

Jason Bull is also a member of Pixel Fist, with Gareth Greenall, Lorne and MC Stapleton. Discogs still lists Colin Worth and Jason Bull as the two members of the name.

Warning: recent records

Dirty Kitchen Rave is where the recent records sit, including ACIDICA with Miss Mants and JUMP with DJ Brownie (both 2025). On the Optimal Breaks calendar he plays Pure Bassline’s seventh anniversary in Seville and Olibass Open Air in Torredonjimeno, both in 2026.
