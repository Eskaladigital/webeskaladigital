The roots

Afghan Headspin started in 2006 as Mackie (Jason Bull) and Wrisk (Colin Worth), both from Resonant Evil, the early-2000s UK drum & bass trio completed by Audio. The name was a vehicle for breaks, drum & bass, dubstep and whatever else they wanted to make.

The records

Going Down came out on Skint on 9 May 2011, featuring MC Coppa. No One Dies, an eighteen-track album, came out on Soundsystem Bassrock on 25 November 2022. Discogs still lists Colin Worth and Jason Bull as members.

The now

Label manager and A&R at Dirty Kitchen Rave. Breakbeat, UK bass and rave, with D&B and hardcore as the roots, and a strong tie to Spain and Andalusia.
