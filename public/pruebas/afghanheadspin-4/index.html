Afghan Headspin started in 2006 as a project of Mackie (Jason Bull) and Wrisk (Colin Worth). Both came from Resonant Evil, the early-2000s British drum & bass trio completed by Audio (Gareth Greenall). Going Down came out on Skint on 9 May 2011, featuring MC Coppa. No One Dies, an eighteen-track album, came out on Soundsystem Bassrock on 25 November 2022. Discogs still lists both founders as members.

Also: Jason Bull is a member of Pixel Fist, with Gareth Greenall, Lorne and MC Stapleton.
