The label

A multi-genre bass label for bassheads, ravers and late-night misfits, built to break genre rules rather than follow them.

230+catalogue numbers

DKR is run from London by label manager Afghan Headspin. The roster brings together UK producers with a large international crew, many from the Spanish breaks scene.

Releases range from 134 BPM breaks to half-time UK garage, liquid drum & bass, jungle compilations and rave anthems, with remix packages and the long-running Universal Breaks and WE ARE DKR series.

The label also runs a podcast, live streams on Twitch, a Discord community, its own merch line and vinyl pressings.
