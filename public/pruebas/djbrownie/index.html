🍫DJ Brownie
5 homepage proposals

Thirty years of breaks, baked in layers

Five visual directions for DJ Brownie's new website. They all tell the same story (who he is, his career, his releases and how to book him) with a different personality. Four of them actually make sound. Open each one, have a play and tell us which feels most like you.

01With sound

Sequencer

The homepage is a drum machine that really plays, at 135 bpm in E♭ minor, the key of "Effect". Five pads, five kinds of breakbeat: funk, old school, nu-skool, a chopped edit and dark UK bass.

Open demo →
02Scratch it

After-hours turntable

Sodium streetlight over black, straight out of an LA after-hours. A working deck: grab the record and scratch, forwards and backwards. Releases and aliases are sleeves that flip over.

Open demo →
03Interactive

Brownie FM

A 70s hi-fi receiver in a walnut case. Switch it on, turn the dial and find seven stations hidden in the static, each one a chapter with its own break. Booking is a radio schedule.

Open demo →
04Playable

Arcade · Break Hero

He scores video games, so his site is an arcade cabinet. A playable rhythm game where you drum the break, his aliases as selectable players, his career as a world map and a "Continue?" to book.

Open demo →
05Editorial

Layers

A 3D brownie that follows your cursor. Every layer is a stage of his career, from the after-hours base to the "Effect" glaze. Click one and it slides out like a drawer. The bio is a recipe.
