    About

    From Resonant Evil
    to KILLA

    Afghan Headspin is Jason Bull. The name started in 2006 as a project of Mackie (Jason Bull) and Wrisk (Colin Worth), both from Resonant Evil, the early-2000s British drum & bass trio completed by Audio. When the trio stopped working as a regular group, Mackie and Wrisk opened Afghan Headspin for breaks, drum & bass, dubstep and whatever else they wanted to make.

    Going Down came out on Skint on 9 May 2011, billed as Afghan Headspin featuring MC Coppa. On 25 November 2022 Soundsystem Bassrock released No One Dies, an eighteen-track album.

    Jason Bull is also a member of Pixel Fist, with Gareth Greenall, Lorne and MC Stapleton. Discogs still lists Colin Worth and Jason Bull as the two members of the name.

    Today he is label manager and A&R at Dirty Kitchen Rave, the centre of the recent work. The records sit in breakbeat, UK bass and rave; drum & bass and hardcore are the roots. And the project has a strong tie to Spain, Andalusia in particular.
